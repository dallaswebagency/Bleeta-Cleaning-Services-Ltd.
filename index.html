Everything you need to know about commercial cleaning in Calgary with Bleeta.

Bleeta prices office cleaning by square foot per clean. Small offices (500–2,000 sq ft) start at $0.14/sq ft per clean, and mid-size offices (2,000–6,000 sq ft) are priced at $0.12/sq ft. A 2,000 sq ft office cleaned weekly works out to approximately $240–$280 per clean, billed monthly. Flat-rate pricing so you always know your cost.

Yes. All Bleeta office cleaning services are set up as 3-month minimum recurring contracts with monthly invoicing. Daily, 3x per week, weekly, and bi-weekly schedules available. Same dedicated crew every visit, no rotating strangers. After the initial 3-month term, contracts continue month-to-month.

Drywall dust is ultra-fine silica particulate that penetrates surfaces, HVAC systems, and vents. Standard vacuums spread drywall dust rather than removing it, recirculating it through the air. Bleeta uses HEPA-filtered vacuuming first, followed by damp wipe-down in the correct sequence to fully remove dust. This is why Calgary drywall contractors rely on us for 10+ homes per day.

Bleeta prices post-construction cleaning at $0.35/sq ft for standard projects and $0.28/sq ft for multi-unit projects of 5+ units. A 3,000 sq ft commercial space would be approximately $1,050 flat-rate. Includes full debris removal, drywall dust treatment, window cleaning, floor scrub and polish, and final detail pass. Quotes within 24 hours.

Yes. Bleeta carries full commercial liability insurance. Proof of insurance available on request, required by most property managers and general contractors. We're a locally owned Calgary business, not a franchise.

We respond to all quote requests within 24 hours. After confirmation, we schedule a quick walkthrough and can typically start your first clean within 5–7 business days. For urgent post-construction cleans, same-week scheduling is often available depending on crew availability.
